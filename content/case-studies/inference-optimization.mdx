## System Architecture

Three-layer optimization: model-level (quantization), request-level (batching), and infrastructure-level (autoscaling).

### Quantization Analysis

Evaluated three quantization approaches on our 13B parameter model:

| Method | Throughput | Quality (eval score) | Memory | Notes |
|--------|-----------|---------------------|--------|-------|
| FP16 (baseline) | 1.0× | 100% | 26 GB | Current production |
| INT8 (GPTQ) | 1.8× | 98.8% | 14 GB | Best quality/perf ratio |
| INT4 (AWQ) | 2.4× | 95.1% | 8 GB | Too much degradation |
| FP8 (TensorRT) | 2.1× | 99.2% | 14 GB | Requires H100s |

Chose **INT8 with GPTQ** for the 13B model. The 1.2% quality loss was within our 5% budget, and the 1.8× throughput improvement was significant. Combined with continuous batching, total throughput reached 3.2×.

### Continuous Batching with vLLM

Migrated from naive single-request serving to vLLM's PagedAttention and continuous batching. Key configuration decisions:

```python
# vLLM engine config
engine_args = EngineArgs(
    model="our-model-13b-gptq",
    quantization="gptq",
    max_num_batched_tokens=8192,
    max_num_seqs=256,
    gpu_memory_utilization=0.90,
    enforce_eager=False,  # Use CUDA graphs
)
```

### Key Decisions & Tradeoffs

1. **vLLM over TensorRT-LLM**: TensorRT-LLM offered 15% better raw throughput but required model-specific compilation and had a much harder deployment story. vLLM's flexibility to serve multiple model sizes on the same codebase was decisive.

2. **Autoscaling strategy**: Implemented predictive autoscaling based on historical traffic patterns (cron-based pre-scaling) combined with reactive scaling on queue depth. This reduced cold-start latency from 3 minutes to 0 during known traffic spikes.

3. **Quality monitoring**: Deployed a shadow evaluation pipeline that continuously samples 1% of production traffic, runs it through the eval suite, and alerts if quality drops below threshold. This caught a tokenizer mismatch bug that would have degraded 7B model outputs.

## Eval Methodology

- **Offline eval**: Full eval suite (800 cases) run against quantized models before deployment
- **A/B testing**: 5% canary traffic to new serving infrastructure, comparing latency, throughput, and response quality
- **Production monitoring**: Real-time dashboards tracking tokens/second, queue depth, GPU utilization, and quality scores from shadow eval
