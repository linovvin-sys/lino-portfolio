## What it does

Lampara helps people get from where they are to where they need to go.

- **AR navigation**: point the phone's camera ahead and arrows and markers are drawn on top of the real world to show the route.
- **AI conversational navigator**: ask things like *"Where is the registrar?"* or *"How do I get to the library?"* and the assistant understands the question and starts the route.
- **Admin-managed locations**: rooms, offices and landmarks are stored in a database, so new places can be added without changing the app.

## How it works

1. The user asks a question or picks a destination.
2. The AI assistant matches the request to a known location.
3. The app looks up the route to that location.
4. AR markers guide the user step by step until they arrive.
