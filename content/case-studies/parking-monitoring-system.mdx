## Features

- **Slot map**: a grid of parking slots colored by status (free / occupied)
- **Vehicle entry**: record plate number and time, assign a slot
- **Vehicle exit**: free the slot and compute the fee from the time parked
- **History**: a searchable log of all entries and exits for reports

## How it's built

The app follows an object-oriented design in Java, with classes for slots, vehicles and parking records, a Swing interface, and MySQL for storage.
